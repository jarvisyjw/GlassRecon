Enhancing Glass Surface Reconstruction via Depth Prior for Robot Navigation

An RGB-D camera can measure the room beyond a glass door and leave the door out of the map. GlassRecon uses the RGB image and nearby depth measurements to recover the glass surface.

Method

  1. Predict relative depth

    DA3-LARGE takes each RGB frame and predicts the relative depth of surfaces in the scene.

  2. Recover metric scale

    GlassRecon samples patches of valid sensor depth. A RANSAC-based fit selects a scale and shift that agree with measurements across the frame.

  3. Fuse the sequence

    In the ScanNet++ runs, GlassRecon refines depth over five frames. nvblox fuses the metric depths and RGB using the supplied COLMAP poses.

Planning around a glass door

On a campus sequence, we converted each reconstruction into a 2D occupancy map and ran A*. The route on the GlassRecon map goes around the door. On the raw-sensor map it crosses the glass.

Glass can still disappear from the map when the depth model sees the background through it.

GlassRecon dataset

The benchmark contains 917 indoor RGB-D images with glass masks, camera intrinsics and reference depths. We fit planes to annotated points on surfaces such as window frames to recover depth inside each glass mask.
